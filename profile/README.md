## 🚀 About Team Kaube

**Team Kaube** is our space for building, experimenting and collaborating on software projects.

Here you'll find everything from small experiments and prototypes to larger applications, developer tools and reusable components.

We like working with modern technologies, trying new ideas and actually building things rather than just talking about them.
